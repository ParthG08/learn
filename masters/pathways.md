# Master's Pathways

## Two Tracks

### Track 1: Master's in Japan (Computational Science)

Strong alignment with Chemical Engineering background via HPC/simulation. Cheaper tuition, clear path to Japan PR via HSP visa. Regardless of which track you choose, *continuous Japanese study is mandatory* — JLPT N2/N1 is essential to keep Japan as a reliable backup for a stable SDE job.

**Top Programs in Japan:**

- **University of Tokyo (UTokyo)** — Dept. of Creative Informatics, Sugiyama-Ishida Lab (ML +自然科学), Yoshimoto Lab (Computational Chemistry)
- **Science Tokyo (formerly Tokyo Tech)** — School of Computing, Matsuoka Lab (HPC/GSIC)
- **Kyoto University** — Graduate School of Informatics, Kikumoto Lab (Computational Science / Numerical Simulation)
- **Tohoku University** — Graduate School of Information Sciences (GSIS), Applied Information Sciences / Complex Systems
- **Osaka University** — Graduate School of Information Science and Technology, Computational Science
- **Nagoya University** — Graduate School of Informatics, Computational Science & Engineering
- **Waseda University** — Graduate School of Fundamental Science and Engineering, Computational Science
- **Keio University** — Graduate School of Science and Technology, Computational Science / HPC

### Track 2: Master's in US (Computational Science)

Globally recognized degrees, strong research ecosystems, higher upfront cost. Same Japanese study requirement applies — JLPT progress keeps Japan as a safety net for SDE roles after graduation.

**Top Programs in US:**

- **MIT** — Computational Science and Engineering (CSE) via Center for Computational Engineering
- **Stanford University** — Institute for Computational and Mathematical Engineering (ICME), MS in Computational & Mathematical Engineering
- **Carnegie Mellon University** — MS in Computational Science and Engineering (CSE)
- **UC Berkeley** — Master of Engineering in Computational Science & Engineering
- **University of Chicago** — MS in Computational Science
- **University of Texas at Austin** — MS in Computational Science, Engineering, and Mathematics (CSEM)
- **University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign** — MS in Computer Science (Computational Science focus) / CSE
- **Georgia Tech** — MS in Computational Science and Engineering (CSE)
- **Cornell University** — MS in Computational Science and Engineering
- **University of Washington** — MS in Computational Science / Scientific Computing

## Key Considerations

| Factor | Japan | US |
|---|---|---|
| **Tuition (annual)** | ~¥500K–1M ($3.5K–7K) | ~$30K–60K |
| **Duration** | 2 years | 1.5–2 years |
| **Language** | Programs in English, but JLTP needed for life/PR | English only |
| **PR Pathway** | HSP visa → PR in 1–3 years | H1B (lottery), then green card (long queue) |
| **Industry Fit** | Strong in robotics, manufacturing, HPC | Strong in tech/software, AI/ML |
| **Backup SDE Market** | Japan SDE market accessible with JLTP N2+ | US SDE market competitive for internationals |

> **Bottom line:** Both tracks require rigorous Japanese study. Japan path is cheaper and leads to faster PR/security. US path offers global brand/prestige but is more expensive and riskier for immigration. Continue JLPT prep regardless of which you choose.
