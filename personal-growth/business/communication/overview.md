# Communication Skills for FDE Role

## Purpose of This Subdirectory

This subdirectory contains comprehensive notes on **communication and interpersonal skills** essential for a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) role, particularly in Japan. FDEs work at the intersection of engineering and customer success — strong communication is as critical as technical skills.

---

## Content Structure (What Should Be Written Here)

### 1. **Customer-Facing Communication**
- Writing clear technical explanations for non-technical stakeholders
- Handling customer escalations and concerns
- Building trust and credibility with clients
- Examples and case studies

### 2. **Stakeholder Management**
- Coordinating across teams (engineering, sales, operations, finance)
- Managing expectations and timelines
- Reporting and status updates
- Conflict resolution

### 3. **Japanese Business Communication** ⭐ (Critical for Japan FDE)
- Japanese business etiquette and cultural norms
- Hierarchy and respectful communication
- Common business phrases and formal vs. informal language
- Email etiquette in Japanese business context
- Meeting protocols (keigo, decision-making culture)
- Networking and relationship-building (nemawashi)

### 4. **Technical Communication & Documentation**
- Writing RFCs and design documents
- Creating technical specifications
- Documentation best practices
- Presenting architecture and technical decisions

### 5. **Presentation & Speaking Skills**
- Demo preparation and delivery
- Technical talks and knowledge sharing
- Presentation structure and storytelling
- Handling Q&A sessions
- Remote vs. in-person communication

### 6. **Written Communication**
- Email communication best practices
- Slack/team chat etiquette
- Technical blog posts and thought leadership
- Incident reports and post-mortems

### 7. **Soft Skills & Emotional Intelligence**
- Active listening
- Empathy and perspective-taking
- Giving and receiving feedback
- Emotional regulation under pressure

### 8. **Remote & Async Communication**
- Working across time zones (relevant for Japan + global teams)
- Async communication patterns
- Video call best practices
- Documentation-driven culture

---

## How to Use This Directory

- Create individual `.md` files for each topic above (e.g., `customer-facing-communication.md`, `japanese-business-communication.md`, etc.)
- Update this `overview.md` with links to completed notes
- Reference real examples and scenarios from your FDE journey
- Regularly review and refine as you gain experience with customers
