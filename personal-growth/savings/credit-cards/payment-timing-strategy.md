# The Smartest Strategy

Instead of paying after every single transaction:

1. Use your card normally throughout the month.
2. A couple of days before your cycle date (e.g., Aug 12–13), pay down most of the balance—leaving just 5%–10% of your total limit unpaid.
3. Let the statement generate on August 15th with that small remaining balance.
4. Pay off that small statement balance before the due date (Sept 2–4).
