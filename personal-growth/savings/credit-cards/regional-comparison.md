# US Travel Credit Cards vs The World

US travel credit cards are the most generous in the world. Nothing in Europe, UK, Asia, or South America comes close.

## The Economic Reason: High Interchange Fees

Credit card rewards are funded by **interchange fees** — the swipe fee banks charge merchants per transaction.

| Region | Interchange Fee Cap | Effect on Rewards |
|---|---|---|
| EU / UK | 0.3% (capped by law) | Banks make almost nothing → no air miles, no big sign-up bonuses, no lounge access |
| Australia / Asia | 0.5%–1.5% (mostly capped) | Modest rewards |
| **United States** | **No cap** (2.0%–3.5% charged) | Banks extract billions from merchants → compete fiercely for spenders with insane travel perks |

## Why It Matters

US banks extract hundreds of billions from merchants every year via high interchange fees. They pump that money right back into travel perks to compete for high-spending consumers — creating a rewards ecosystem no other region can match.
