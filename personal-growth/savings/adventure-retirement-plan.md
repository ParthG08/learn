# Adventure Retirement Plan — Funding a 5–8 Year World Tour by 40

Synthesis of a planning conversation about funding an experience-maximalist, budget-conscious world tour after leaving the FDE career. Goal: **retire around 40 and travel continuously for 5–8 years** without worrying about money, hitting every country's experiences (including extreme sports) without spending blindly.

---

## 1. The Lifestyle Definition

- **Not luxury travel** — planned, deliberate, high-coverage travel.
- **Not checklist tourism either** — want *all experiences a country has to offer* (moonlight parties in Thailand, scuba diving, wing walking, base jumping, etc.).
- Max area coverage per trip (whole Southeast Asia in one planned sweep, South America similarly).
- If a normal person can afford an experience, I want to do it.

## 2. The Budget Math

| Budget level | What it covers |
| :--- | :--- |
| **$20K/year** | Covers most countries + most experiences, especially affordable regions (SEA, parts of South America). Tight for Western Europe / US adventure sports (gear, training, insurance are pricey). |
| **$30K–40K/year** | **Realistic global average.** Comfortably experiences everything without compromising on safety/quality for high-adrenaline activities. |
| **< $30K** | Possible only by sacrificing extreme-sports experiences. **$30K is the practical floor** for this specific lifestyle — base jumping etc. always carry significant gear/training/insurance costs. |

### Nest-egg targets (for a fixed travel window, no perpetual withdrawal math)

| Travel duration | Budget | Total needed |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **5 years** | $30K–40K/yr | **$150K–$200K** |
| **8 years** | $30K–40K/yr | **$240K–$320K** |

### Required savings rate

- $240K over 8 years → **$30K/year saved**
- $300K over 8 years → **$37.5K/year saved**

## 3. Cost-Reduction Levers (all confirmed viable — can cut the budget substantially)

1. **Hostels instead of hotels.**
2. **Planned flight booking** + always choosing the cheapest transport (land travel, buses).
3. **Regional multi-country travel** in single sweeps.
4. **Day jobs while traveling** (yoga teacher, translator, hostel-work exchanges) — can **cut the travel budget need in half or more**.
5. **Forecast on remote work:** by ~40, AI will likely have eroded remote/online income opportunities, so plan on **in-person day jobs**, not remote software work.

## 4. Feasibility by Career Path

| Path | Time to save target |
| :--- | :--- |
| **US-based FDE** | **~2–4 years** (higher comp + stock-based comp, even after higher COL) |
| **Japan-based FDE** | **~5–8+ years** (lower base; depends heavily on company and Tokyo COL) |

**Recommended bridging strategy:** Work FDE in Japan (6–7 years), then move to the US (internal transfer or new role) for 2–3 years to bank the savings — this fits the numbers above comfortably.

## 5. Takeaways / Action Items

- **Adopt $30K–40K/yr as the planning budget**; treat $20K as the frugal-case benchmark.
- Primary target is the **$240K–$320K range** (8-year window).
- Keep the **Japan → US bridge** as the primary funding mechanism; it aligns with the existing career plan.
- Legal note: working while on a tourist visa (even barter) is still considered work — see `../escape/travel-visa-work-rules.md`.
