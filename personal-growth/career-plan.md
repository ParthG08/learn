# Career Plan

**Long-term goal:** Become a high-earning Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) in Japan within 4–5 years.

---

## Current State

- Backend engineer at a startup in Bangalore, India.

---

## 2–3 Year Roadmap

### Phase 1: MNC Transition
- Move from startup to a **software engineering role at an MNC**.
- **Why:** Exposure to how large firms operate, better brand name on resume.

### Phase 2: Japan Entry
- Learn Japanese seriously.
- Switch to a job in Japan — either via:
  - A **master's degree** in Japan, or
  - **Direct job switch** to a company in Japan.

### Phase 3: Language Immersion & Client-Facing Skills
- After landing in Japan, **immerse fully in the language**.
- Reach a level where I can:
  - Communicate directly with clients.
  - Build and maintain client relationships.

### Phase 4: Break Into FDE
- Use combined **language skills + CS/AI skills** built over the years.
- Land an **FDE role** in Japan.

### Phase 5: Post-FDE Paths
- **Option A:** Keep working as an FDE — grow skills, personal brand, and compensation.
- **Option B:** Join brother's company **Turing** (AI solutions company with large Indian clients):
  - Help them expand into the Japanese market.
  - Leverage Japanese language + FDE skills to bring in Japanese clients.

### Phase 6: The Japan → US Bridge (funding the early retirement plan)
- Work as an FDE in Japan for **6–7 years** (build track record + Japanese), then move to the US via **internal transfer** (Palantir, Google, Amazon, Microsoft all support cross-region mobility) or a new application for **2–3 years**.
- US FDE comp lets me save the **$240K–320K** adventure-retirement nest egg in **2–4 years** (vs. 5–8+ years in Japan).
- **Fallback route:** master's in the US (1–2 years of study; see `masters/pathways.md`) — doable but not preferred.
- **Adjacent communication-heavy roles** (AI-resistant, natural FDE exit): Solutions Architect, Sales Engineer, Technical Account Manager. See `forward-deployed-engineer/japan-vs-us-fde-strategy.md`.
